# OVERVIEW

This document summarizes the standard required documents for various community services and schemes. Note that individual schemes may have additional specific requirements.

# IDENTITY PROOF

Acceptable identity proofs include:
- National ID Card (Aadhaar, Passport)
- Community Membership Card (valid and unexpired)
- Driving License

# RESIDENCE PROOF

Acceptable residence proofs include:
- Utility bill (not older than 3 months)
- Rent agreement
- Domicile certificate

# INCOME CERTIFICATE

For schemes requiring financial evaluation, you must provide:
- Official income certificate issued by the government OR
- Last 3 months bank statements and a self-declaration letter.

# SUBMISSION RULES

- All documents must be uploaded in PDF or JPG format.
- Maximum file size per document is 5MB.
- Ensure documents are clear and legible; blurry documents will result in application rejection.
