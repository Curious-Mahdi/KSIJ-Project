------------------------------------------------
EDUCATION SUPPORT SCHEME
Version: 2.1
Effective From: 01 September 2026
Authority: Community Welfare Department
Category: Education Scheme
------------------------------------------------

# OVERVIEW

The Education Support Scheme provides financial
support to eligible students from participating
communities.

# ELIGIBILITY

Applicants must:
- Be residents of Maharashtra.
- Be enrolled in an approved educational institution.
- Meet the household income requirement.

# REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

Applicants must submit:
- Identity proof
- Residence proof
- Income certificate
- Enrollment certificate

# APPLICATION PROCESS

1. Complete the online application.
2. Upload required documents.
3. Submit the application.
4. Track verification status.

# IMPORTANT CONDITIONS

Applications with incomplete documentation
may be rejected or returned for correction.
