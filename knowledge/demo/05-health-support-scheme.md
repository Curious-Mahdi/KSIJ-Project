------------------------------------------------
HEALTH SUPPORT SCHEME
Version: 1.0
Effective From: 01 May 2025
Authority: Community Welfare Department
Category: Health Scheme
------------------------------------------------

# OVERVIEW

The Health Support Scheme provides financial assistance to members undergoing major medical treatments.

# ELIGIBILITY

Applicants must:
- Be an active member for at least 2 years.
- Have a verified medical diagnosis requiring surgery or long-term hospitalization.
- Not have claimed under this scheme in the past 12 months.

# REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

Applicants must submit:
- Identity proof
- Medical reports and doctor's prescription
- Hospital admission estimate
- Bank account details

# APPLICATION PROCESS

1. Download the health claim form.
2. Get the form signed by the attending doctor.
3. Submit the form with all required documents to the welfare office.
4. An approval committee reviews it within 7 days.

# IMPORTANT CONDITIONS

Only treatments at recognized hospitals are eligible for the maximum payout.
