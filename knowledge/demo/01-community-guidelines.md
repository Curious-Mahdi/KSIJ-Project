# OVERVIEW

These guidelines govern the behavior and participation of all members within our community platform.

# CONDUCT

Members are expected to:
- Treat everyone with respect.
- Not post spam or irrelevant promotional content.
- Support fellow members in community initiatives.

# DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS

Violations of the conduct policy can lead to:
1. A formal warning.
2. Temporary suspension from the platform.
3. Permanent ban in severe cases.

# GRIEVANCES

If you experience an issue with another member, please refer to the Grievance Procedure (Document 08).
