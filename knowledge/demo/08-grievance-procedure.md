# OVERVIEW

The Grievance Procedure outlines the steps to resolve disputes or file complaints regarding community services.

# STEPS TO FILE A GRIEVANCE

1. Initial Complaint: Submit a written complaint via the community portal under "Help & Support".
2. Review: The grievance officer reviews the complaint within 3 business days.
3. Mediation: If necessary, a mediation session is scheduled.
4. Resolution: A formal decision is communicated within 14 days of the initial complaint.

# REQUIRED INFORMATION

When filing, provide:
- Your full name and member ID.
- Date and time of the incident.
- Names of involved parties.
- A clear description of the issue.

# APPEAL

If unsatisfied with the resolution, you may file an appeal to the Community Board within 30 days of the decision.
